# BevAlc Intelligence Commercial Taxonomy

This document defines the standardized product classification system used to categorize every COLA filing. When Claude processes a filing, it must assign a `super_category`, `category`, and `subcategory` from the values listed below. No other values are permitted.

Last updated: 2026-02-13
Version: 1.0 (DRAFT - needs domain expert review)

---

## How to read this document

- **Super Category**: The top level (3 values: Spirits, Wine, Beer & FMB)
- **Category**: The product family (~30 values)
- **Subcategory**: The specific commercial style (~150+ values)

Every COLA filing gets exactly ONE super_category, ONE category, and ONE subcategory.

## CLASSIFICATION GUIDANCE FOR EDGE CASES

These notes help Claude resolve ambiguous filings:

### Whiskey-Based Cream Liqueurs
If the primary identity is the liqueur (e.g., "Irish Cream"), classify under **Liqueur & Cordial → Cream Liqueur**, not under the whiskey category.

### Flavored Spirits vs. Liqueurs
If ABV is 30%+ and the base spirit is clearly identified (e.g., "Honey Bourbon"), classify under the spirit's flavored subcategory (e.g., **Bourbon → Flavored Bourbon**). If ABV is under 30% or the product identity is the flavor rather than the base spirit, classify under **Liqueur & Cordial**.

### RTD Cocktails vs. FMBs
If the product is spirits-based (contains actual distilled spirits), classify under **Ready-to-Drink Spirits (RTD)**. If the product is malt-based but designed to taste like a cocktail, classify under **Flavored Malt Beverage (FMB) → Spirit-Flavored FMB**.

### Hard Seltzers
If filed as a malt beverage with neutral flavor profile and low calories, classify under **Hard Seltzer** regardless of whether it's technically malt-based or spirits-based.

### Sake
If filed as wine (which it often is in TTB classifications), still classify under **Sake & Asian Spirits** based on the product identity.

### Cider
TTB classifies cider as wine. Regardless of TTB classification, if the product is apple-based and marketed as cider, classify under **Beer & FMB → Hard Cider**.

### Vermouth
TTB classifies vermouth as wine. Classify under **Wine → Vermouth & Aromatized Wine**.

### Mead
TTB may classify mead as wine. Classify under **Wine → Fruit & Non-Grape Wine → Mead**.

### Non-Alcoholic Products
If a product is filed with TTB but is non-alcoholic or de-alcoholized, classify under the appropriate NA category. Non-alcoholic spirits (e.g., Seedlip) should get **Spirits → Specialty & Other Spirits → Specialty Spirit (Other)** with a note.

### Unknown / Unclassifiable
If Claude cannot determine the category with reasonable confidence, it should assign the most specific category it can and use the "(Other)" subcategory. The confidence field should be set to "low".

## REVISION NOTES

This taxonomy should be reviewed and updated:
- When new product categories emerge (e.g., if "hard water" becomes a category)
- When filing patterns show a subcategory needs to be split (e.g., if "Flavored Vodka" volume warrants breaking into citrus, berry, etc.)
- When industry terminology evolves
- Quarterly at minimum during the first year of operation

Categories with fewer than 50 filings per year may be candidates for consolidation into "(Other)" subcategories. Categories with more than 5,000 filings per year may be candidates for further subdivision.
